# Differentiators: why use it instead of Language Reactor

Language Reactor is free, established and already does dual subtitles and click-to-translate on YouTube and Netflix. Matching it feature for feature is not a reason to switch. Differentiators matter for the portfolio story (D-061). After the competitor review, only **1** is distinctive: FluentAI already shows known % per video, Lingopie already has clip-based flashcards and grammar tags.

1. **Your library is searchable.** Search every video you've added for every moment a word or phrase was said.
2. **Cards remember where they came from.** Every card plays a clip from one of the word's saved moments. The connections list shows every moment a saved word appears across your library.
3. **Known-word coverage per video.** The share of a video's words you already know, shown on the video you have open. This measures vocabulary coverage, not comprehension.
4. **Grammar view** for German. *Not yet defined: what it shows and how it works is decided in the feature discussion.*

If a feature doesn't strengthen one of these, question it.
