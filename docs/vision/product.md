# Product

## What LinguaStream is

A web app plus a browser extension for learning a language from YouTube videos. You watch with dual subtitles, click any word for an instant translation and dictionary data, save words to flashcards linked to the exact clip, and review them with spaced repetition. Words are colored by how well you know them, and a video shows what share of its words you already know. Your own video files work too: they play from your disk, and only the audio is sent for transcription and then deleted (D-040).

## Who it's for

Intermediate learners (roughly A2–B2) who already learn from native content and want their vocabulary work tied to what they watch. Starting with German, then English.

## Non-goals

- No AI chat or "ask the video" features.
- No technology added only to list it on a CV. Every component must have an answer to "why is this here?"
