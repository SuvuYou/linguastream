# Product

## What LinguaStream is

**A portfolio and learning project used by a few friends (D-061).** When goals conflict, a small, well-built core and a strong backend story win over product breadth.

A web app plus a browser extension for learning a language from YouTube videos. You watch with dual subtitles, click any word for an instant translation and dictionary data, save words to flashcards linked to the exact clip, and review them with spaced repetition. Words are colored by how well you know them, and a video shows what share of its words you already know. Uploading your own video files is designed (D-040) but postponed (D-066).

## Who it's for

Intermediate learners (roughly A2–B2) who already learn from native content and want their vocabulary work tied to what they watch. Starting with German, then English.

## Non-goals

- No AI chat or "ask the video" features. Exception: a one-shot "Explain" for a line or word (D-044).
- No decorative technology. Learning is a legitimate reason to use a technology, but each one is used properly and has an answer to "why is this here?" (D-061).
