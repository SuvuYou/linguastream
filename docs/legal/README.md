# Legal guardrails

Rules every change must respect. They exist to avoid copyright and privacy warning letters (Abmahnungen) and to keep the legal basis described in `decisions/` (D-008, D-009, D-014, D-015) intact.

**Not legal advice, and no lawyer has reviewed this.** The reasoning is the developer's own risk assessment from research. Unverified points are in `legal/unverified-assumptions.md`. If a change would break a rule here, stop and raise it. Don't work around it.

Rules are numbered across files (1 to 29), so references such as "guardrails 12–15" stay valid.
