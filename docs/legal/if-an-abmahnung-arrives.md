# If an Abmahnung arrives

1. Note the deadline (often 7 to 14 days). Do not ignore it: silence invites a court injunction.
2. Do **not** sign the enclosed Unterlassungserklaerung as drafted; it carries a contractual penalty for every later breach.
3. See an IT/copyright lawyer the same day.
4. Stop the disputed conduct at once (remove the named video by ID, or switch the feature off) and record what was done and when.
5. A plain email asking for removal is answered fast and professionally; that is usually the end of it.
